# Partner Outreach Kit

Ready-to-send pitches for turning local businesses into listed partners.
The play (from reviewer feedback): offer a business a free AI audit of their
own documents → they fix what's flagged → they get listed as a verified
partner on ScrewedScore → you get a credibility anchor + local word of mouth.

Send from your normal email. Keep it personal — one business per email,
name them specifically. The /for-businesses page does the heavy lifting
once they're interested.

---

## Template 1 — Housing complex / property manager (the reviewer's example)

**Subject: Free review of your lease agreement — before a tenant's lawyer does it**

Hi [name],

I run ScrewedScore ([www.screwedscore.com](https://www.screwedscore.com)), an AI tool people use to
check bills and contracts for problems. Renters are one of our biggest user
groups — and lease agreements are one of the most-scanned documents.

Here's my offer: send me your standard lease and I'll run our full analysis
on it for free. You'll see exactly what a tenant (or their lawyer) would
flag — unenforceable clauses, missing protections, anything that could bite
you in a dispute. No strings attached.

If you tighten it up, we'll list [property name] as a ScrewedScore Verified
Partner — meaning when local renters scan a lease, yours is the standard
we point to. It's free marketing to exactly the people choosing where to rent.

Worth 10 minutes?

[your name]

ScrewedScore Verified shop — so when locals scan an invoice from you, they
see the badge instead of a warning.

Interested?

## Notes

- **First target:** the reviewer specifically suggested a local housing
  complex. Warm intros beat cold email — if you know anyone at a complex,
  start there and use Template 1 as the talking points.
- **Reviews:** when a partner signs on, ask them for a Google review of
  ScrewedScore in the same conversation — they're at peak goodwill right
  after seeing their free audit.
- **Deliver the audit as a Fight Back Kit-style PDF** — it doubles as a demo
  of the paid product.
- Track outreach in a simple sheet: business, contact, date sent, template
  used, response. Volume matters less than follow-up.
