Pokebank chat mod bot
=====================

Removes bad messages. Mutes whoever keeps sending them. Says nothing.

There is nothing to install. No terminal, no Node, no API key.


LOAD IT
-------
0. UNZIP IT FIRST. Chrome cannot load a .zip file.
   Right-click the download -> "Extract All..." -> Extract.

1. Open   chrome://extensions       (Edge: edge://extensions)
2. Turn on "Developer mode"          - top right
3. Click "Load unpacked"
4. Pick the folder you extracted to - the one holding manifest.json.
   If you extracted to your Downloads, that is:

       C:\Users\<you>\Downloads\pokebank-mod-bot

   You are in the right folder when you can SEE manifest.json in it. If
   Chrome says "Manifest file is missing or unreadable", you are one level
   too high - go into the folder until manifest.json is sitting there.


POINT IT AT YOUR CHAT
---------------------
5. Open your live stream's chat
6. In the chat panel: three dots -> "Pop out chat"
7. Leave that little window somewhere you can see it

   It has to stay visible. Chrome slows down timers in hidden windows, and
   finding the menu entry to click depends on them. Minimised will fail.

   The account you are signed in as must be the channel owner or a
   moderator of that chat, or there is no menu to click and nothing can be
   removed. The extension icon tells you if that is the case - see below.


IS IT WORKING?
--------------
Click the extension icon. The line at the top tells you, in words:

  "Watching this chat - 12 messages seen."
        Working. Nothing else to do.

  "No live chat open."
        The chat window is not open, or not a YouTube chat.

  "Found a YouTube tab, but the extension is not running in it."
        Reload that tab. Chrome does not inject into tabs that were
        already open when you loaded the extension.

  "This account cannot moderate this chat."
        The account you are signed in as is a viewer here. YouTube shows
        it no moderation menu, so nothing can be removed. Sign in as the
        channel owner, or have this account made a moderator.

  "Chat window is hidden."
        Bring it back on screen. Chrome slows hidden windows down.

If it says something else, or nothing, send me that line.


TURN IT ON
----------
8. Click the extension icon. It starts in "Dry run".

   Dry run logs what it WOULD remove and touches nothing. Watch one stream
   that way and read the list.

9. Then switch to "Ask me" and post a test message from a second account
   that trips a word. Tap Delete when it appears.

   That is the one step worth not skipping: dry run never opens a message
   menu, so it proves nothing about whether removal works on YOUR account.

10. Once that works, switch to "Auto".

A deletion cannot be undone. That is the whole reason for that order.


WHAT IT DOES BY DEFAULT
-----------------------
Removes  hate, harassment, doxxing, spam and scam links, and swearing.
Mutes    anyone who trips the rules twice - a timeout, which expires.
Never    bans. Raise the ceiling in Settings if you want that.
Never    posts in your chat. Nothing is sent to Claude at all.
Never    touches your own messages, your mods', or anything a mod already
         handled.

Catches f@ck, shiiit, fu(Cyrillic c)k, f.u.c.k and fucking. Misses "fu ck"
on purpose - closing that gap starts flagging "he's hit".

Settings has the word list, an allow list for false positives, and the
ceiling. Nothing else needs touching.
