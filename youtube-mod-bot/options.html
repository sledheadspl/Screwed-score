Standing rules (below) act immediately in every mode except dry run — the guide's "act first, explain after".

Standing rules — Section 2

Hate speech, harassment, doxxing and spam links. These fire for everyone, no approval, and are checked before the allow list. Patterns for harassment, doxxing and spam ship built in; hate speech ships empty on purpose — add your terms below, or leave that to YouTube's own blocked-words list.

Judgment calls — Section 3

Matched on whole words, tolerant of leetspeak (sh1t, f@ck), stretched letters and leading symbols ($hit).

Covers judgment calls only — a standing-rule violation is still removed.
