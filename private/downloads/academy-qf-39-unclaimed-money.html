Find Unclaimed Money in Your Name

Search official state databases for forgotten deposits, refunds and checks, and claim them free.

Time: 20 to 45 minutesLevel: BeginnerSteps: 4

States hold billions in unclaimed property: old bank accounts, uncashed paychecks, utility deposits, insurance payouts and refunds. Searching and claiming through the official state site is free. You never need to pay someone to search for you.

Read this first

  • Only use official government sites. Beware look-alike sites and anyone asking for a fee to search.
  • Never pay an upfront fee to claim your own money.
  • Expect identity checks; that's normal on official sites.

What you need

  • Every name you've used, and every state you've lived or worked in
  • Old addresses
  • ID and proof of address for claims

Step 1Search the official sites

  1. Go to unclaimed.org (run by the national association of state administrators) and pick each state you've lived in; it links to each official site.
  2. MissingMoney.com searches many participating states at once, but also check each state's own site directly.
  3. Search your name, maiden or previous names, and common misspellings.

Step 2Search for family and your business

  1. Search for deceased relatives if you're an heir; claims usually need a death certificate and proof of heirship.
  2. Search any business you owned, including ones that closed.

Step 3Claim it

  1. Select each matching property and submit the claim online.
  2. Upload ID and proof of address as asked.
  3. Note the claim number and check status every few weeks; processing times vary by state.

Step 4Other places to look

  1. Savings bonds: TreasuryDirect's Treasury Hunt.
  2. Unclaimed federal tax refunds: contact the IRS.
  3. Pensions from former employers: the PBGC's search (for certain plans).
  4. Failed bank deposits: the FDIC's unclaimed funds search.
