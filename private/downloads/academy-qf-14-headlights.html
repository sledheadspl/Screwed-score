Headlight Bulbs and Foggy Lens Restoration

Swap a burned-out bulb and clear yellowed lenses without paying dealer rates.

Time: 20 to 60 minutesLevel: BeginnerSteps: 3

A dim or dead headlight is a ticket and a safety risk. Most halogen bulbs swap from behind the light in minutes. Yellowed plastic lenses can cut light output badly, and a sanding kit restores them for less than one shop visit.

A shop or dealer charges
$80 to $200
You'll spend
$15 to $40

Safety first, read before you start

  • Let bulbs cool before touching. Halogen bulbs get very hot.
  • Don't touch a halogen bulb's glass with bare fingers. Skin oil shortens its life.
  • HID (xenon) headlights use high voltage. If your car has HID, leave bulb replacement to a pro unless the manual shows a safe owner procedure.
  • Wear a mask and work in ventilation when sanding and using lens sealant.

  • Find your bulb type

  • Restore cloudy lenses with a sanding kit

What you need

  • Correct bulb (in the owner's manual or parts lookup)
  • Gloves or a clean cloth
  • Headlight restoration kit (sandpaper, polish, UV sealant)
  • Painter's tape
  • Spray bottle of water

Step 1Replace a halogen bulb

  1. Open the hood and find the back of the headlight housing. Some cars need the air box or a wheel-liner flap moved for access.
  2. Unplug the connector, then twist the bulb a quarter turn counterclockwise (or release its wire clip) and pull it straight out.
  3. Holding the new bulb by its base, insert it in the same orientation, lock it in, and plug in the connector.
  4. Test low and high beam before closing everything up.
Pro tip: Replace bulbs in pairs. The other one is the same age and usually fails soon after.

Step 2Restore yellowed lenses

  1. Wash the lens and mask the paint around it with two layers of painter's tape.
  2. Wet-sand in the order the kit gives (coarse to fine), keeping the surface wet, until the haze is uniformly frosted.
  3. Polish until clear.
  4. Apply the UV sealant exactly as directed. Without it, the yellowing returns within months.
Caution: Sand only the lens. Paint near the edges scratches instantly; that's what the tape is for.

Step 3Check the aim

  1. Park on level ground about 25 feet from a wall, with a normal load and correct tire pressures.
  2. The tops of both low beams' bright zones should be level with each other and slightly below headlight height.
  3. If one is clearly off, the adjuster screws are usually on top of the housing; small turns only, and follow your manual.

Troubleshooting

If this happensDo this
New bulb doesn't lightCheck it's fully seated and the connector clicked. Test the other side's bulb in this socket.
Both lights out at onceFuse or relay, not bulbs. See the fuses lesson.
Lens hazy after restoringNot sanded evenly or polish skipped. Repeat the last two grits and polish.

When to stop and call a pro

  • Car has HID or LED headlights
  • Water inside the housing
  • Housing broken or bulbs need bumper removal

Printable checklist

  • Correct bulb type
  • Glass not touched
  • Bulb locked and connector clicked
  • Low and high beam tested
  • Lens masked before sanding
  • UV sealant applied
  • Aim checked
