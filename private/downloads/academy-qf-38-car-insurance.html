Lower Your Car Insurance in 30 Minutes

Shop your policy properly, find missing discounts and avoid cutting the wrong coverage.

Time: 30 to 60 minutesLevel: BeginnerSteps: 4

Insurance prices change at renewal, and loyalty is not always rewarded. Comparing identical coverage across insurers and checking discounts is the biggest lever. Cutting liability coverage to save money is usually a mistake.

Read this first

  • Never let a policy lapse. Start the new one before cancelling the old.
  • Compare the same coverages and deductibles, or the comparison is meaningless.
  • Be accurate on applications; misstatements can void claims.

What you'll learn

  • Read your declarations page

  • Find discounts you're missing
  • Decide on deductibles and older-car coverage

What you need

  • Your current declarations page
  • Driver's license numbers and VINs
  • Mileage estimate

Step 1Know what you have

  1. Write down liability limits (for example 100/300/100), collision and comprehensive deductibles, and extras.
  2. Note the 6- or 12-month premium.

Step 2Get comparable quotes

  1. Get at least three quotes with exactly the same limits and deductibles.
  2. Include a mix: national insurers and a local independent agent who quotes several companies.

Step 3Ask for discounts

  1. Bundling home/renters, good driver, low mileage, paid-in-full, paperless, defensive driving course, good student, anti-theft, telematics programs.
  2. Ask your current insurer to re-rate you with any new discounts before you switch.

Step 4Adjust smartly

  1. Raising the deductible lowers the premium; only do it if you could pay the deductible tomorrow.
  2. On an older car worth little, compare the yearly cost of collision coverage to the car's value.
  3. Keep liability limits strong; that's what protects your savings in a serious crash.

Troubleshooting

If this happensDo this
Quote jumps at the endAsk what changed (driving record, credit-based score in states that allow it, coverage defaults).
Price rose after a claimAsk whether accident forgiveness applies; shop around anyway.

Get extra help if

  • You've had a major violation or claim and quotes are very high: an independent agent
  • A dispute over a claim: your state insurance department

Printable checklist

  • Declarations page reviewed
  • 3+ identical quotes
  • Discounts requested
  • Deductible decision made
  • Liability kept strong
  • New policy active before cancelling old
