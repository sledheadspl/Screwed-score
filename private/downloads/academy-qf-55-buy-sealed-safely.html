Buy Sealed Packs and Boxes Without Getting Scammed

Know MSRP, spot tampered products and buy sealed Pokémon safely.

Time: 15 minutesLevel: BeginnerSteps: 4

Sealed product is where many collectors get burned: resealed packs with the good cards removed, packs searched by weight, and huge markups on new releases. Buying from trusted retailers at or near MSRP is the simplest protection.

A resealed box or scalped price can cost

Read this first

  • Buy from authorized retailers or sellers with long, strong feedback.
  • Be wary of loose packs from unknown sellers; they're easier to tamper with.
  • Pay with buyer protection.

What you need

  • Phone for price checks

Step 1Know the price

  1. Look up MSRP for the product and recent sold prices.
  2. New releases spike, then often settle. Waiting can save a lot.

Step 2Inspect the wrap

  1. Factory shrink is tight and neat; look for loose, bunched or overly shiny wrap and messy seams.
  2. Many boxes have specific logos or patterns on the wrap; compare with genuine photos.

Step 3Inspect packs

  1. Check the crimps at the top and bottom: even, uniform ridges.
  2. Look for glue, wrinkles, or a pack that opens too easily.

Step 4Buy smart

  1. For chase cards, buying the single is usually far cheaper than opening packs hoping for it.
  2. Open product on video for higher-value purchases from individual sellers.
