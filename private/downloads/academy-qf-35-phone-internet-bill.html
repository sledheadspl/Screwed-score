Cut Your Phone and Internet Bill

Audit your bill, call retention, and leave with a lower monthly price in writing.

Time: 30 to 60 minutesLevel: BeginnerSteps: 4

Telecom companies quietly raise prices when promotions end and add fees that creep up over time. The retention department has authority to offer discounts the regular line can't, and having competitor prices ready is your strongest tool.

Read this first

  • Never agree to a new contract term without hearing the total cost and the early termination fee.
  • Ask for every promise in writing: an email, chat transcript or a confirmation number.
  • Don't cancel your old service until the new one is working.

What you need

  • Last 3 bills
  • Your account PIN or passcode
  • Competitor prices for your address (checked online)

Step 1Audit the bill

  1. List each charge: plan, equipment rental, fees, add-ons.
  2. Look for equipment you could own (modem/router rental often costs $10 to $15 a month).
  3. Flag services you don't use: insurance, premium channels, extra lines.
  4. Check whether a promotion recently expired.

Step 2Know your leverage

  1. Check two competitors' prices at your address.
  2. Check new-customer pricing for your own provider; that's your target.

Step 3Call and use the script

  1. Ask for 'cancellations' or 'retention' early in the call.
  2. Say: 'I've been a customer for X years. My bill went up to $Y. [Competitor] offers Z at my address for $W. What can you do to keep me?'
  3. If the offer is weak, say you'll think about it; the second offer is often better.
  4. Ask to remove rental equipment and unused add-ons.
Pro tip: Be polite and patient. The rep's job is to keep you; make it easy for them to give you the discount.

Step 4Lock it in

  1. Get the new monthly total including taxes and fees, the length of the promo, and whether there's a contract.
  2. Ask for an email confirmation or note the reference number.
  3. Check the next bill matches.

Troubleshooting

If this happensDo this
Rep says no discounts existPolitely ask to schedule cancellation; retention offers usually appear then.
Next bill doesn't matchCall with the reference number and ask for a credit.

Escalate if

  • They refuse to honor a written promise: file a complaint with the FCC (fcc.gov/complaints)
  • Fees you never agreed to keep appearing

Printable checklist

  • 3 bills reviewed
  • Fees and rentals flagged
  • Competitor prices in hand
  • Asked for retention
  • New price incl. fees confirmed
  • Written confirmation saved
  • Next bill checked
