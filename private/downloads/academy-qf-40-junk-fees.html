Spot and Fight Junk Fees

Find hidden fees before you pay and get them waived or refunded after.

Time: 20 to 40 minutesLevel: BeginnerSteps: 4

Junk fees are charges added late in the buying process or buried in bills: resort and service fees, processing fees, overdraft and late fees, 'admin' fees. Many are negotiable, and some new rules require upfront pricing in certain industries.

Read this first

  • Check the final total before you pay, not the advertised price.
  • Ask for fee waivers politely and in writing where possible.
  • Report deceptive pricing; regulators track complaints.

Step 1Catch them before you pay

  1. Go to the final checkout screen before deciding.
  2. Screenshot the advertised price and the final total.
  3. Ask 'what is the total including all fees?' on the phone or at the counter.

Step 2Get bank fees refunded

  1. Call and ask for a courtesy refund of an overdraft or late fee, especially if it's your first in a while.
  2. Turn on low-balance alerts and opt out of overdraft coverage for debit purchases if you prefer declines to fees.

Step 3Get service fees waived

  1. Ask what the fee covers. If the service wasn't provided (for example, the pool was closed), ask for removal.
  2. Ask the manager; front-line staff often can't, managers often can.
  3. If charged a fee that wasn't disclosed, dispute it with your card issuer.

Step 4Report

  1. Report deceptive fees to the FTC (reportfraud.ftc.gov), bank fees to the CFPB (consumerfinance.gov/complaint), and your state attorney general.
