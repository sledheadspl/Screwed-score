Before You Sign: Contractor Estimates and High-Pressure Sales

Read a quote and contract like a pro and back out of pressure deals legally.

Time: 30 to 60 minutesLevel: BeginnerSteps: 4

Most expensive mistakes happen at signing. Clear scope, payment schedules tied to progress, and comparing quotes line by line prevent most disputes. The FTC's Cooling-Off Rule gives you 3 business days to cancel many sales made at your home or at temporary locations.

Read this first

  • Never sign the same day under pressure. Real offers survive overnight.
  • Avoid paying large deposits upfront; tie payments to completed stages.
  • The Cooling-Off Rule covers many sales at your home of $25 or more and at temporary locations of $130 or more, but not everything. Check exceptions.

What you need

  • At least three written quotes
  • Contractor license numbers
  • A pen to mark up the contract

Step 1A good estimate includes

  1. Detailed scope and materials (brands, models, quantities).
  2. Start and finish dates.
  3. Payment schedule by milestones.
  4. Permits: who gets them.
  5. Warranty on work and cleanup terms.

Step 2Compare quotes

  1. Put them side by side line by line. The cheapest quote may be missing items.
  2. Ask each contractor to price the same scope.

Step 3Red flags

  1. Cash only or large upfront payment.
  2. No license or insurance proof.
  3. Vague scope ('repair as needed').
  4. Pressure to sign today or 'today-only' prices.
  5. Refusal to put promises in writing.

Step 4Cancel within 3 days

  1. If covered by the Cooling-Off Rule, the seller must tell you about your right to cancel and give you a cancellation form.
  2. Send the cancellation before midnight of the third business day; keep proof of mailing.
  3. Some states give extra rights for home improvement or solar contracts; check your state.

Troubleshooting

If this happensDo this
Seller didn't give a cancellation noticeYour cancellation window may be extended; report it to the FTC and your attorney general.
Already paid a depositCancel in writing in time; the seller must generally refund within 10 days under the rule.

Get help if

  • Large projects: have someone experienced review the contract
  • Solar or home loan contracts with financing: read the loan terms carefully or ask a housing counselor

Printable checklist

  • 3 written quotes
  • Same scope priced
  • Licenses verified
  • Milestone payments
  • Promises in writing
  • Slept on it
  • Cancellation rights checked
