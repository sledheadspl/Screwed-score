Get Hospital Bills Reduced With Financial Assistance

Find the hospital's financial assistance policy, apply, and get the bill cut or erased.

Time: 1 to 2 hoursLevel: BeginnerSteps: 3

Nonprofit hospitals in the U.S. are required under federal tax rules to have a written financial assistance policy and make it public. Income limits are often well above the poverty line, and many hospitals accept applications well after the bill arrives, even after it's gone to collections.

Read this first

  • Apply as early as you can, but don't assume it's too late. Many policies accept applications for at least 240 days after the first bill.
  • Ask the hospital to pause collection activity while your application is pending.
  • Keep copies of everything you submit.

What you need

  • Recent pay stubs or tax return
  • Household size
  • The bill(s) and account numbers

Step 1Find the policy

  1. Search the hospital's name plus 'financial assistance policy' or 'charity care'.
  2. Download the policy and the plain-language summary and the application.
  3. Find the income table: it's usually a percentage of the federal poverty guidelines.

Step 2Apply

  1. Fill out the application completely; missing pages are the top reason for delays.
  2. Attach the proof they ask for, and list every account number you want covered.
  3. Ask in writing that collection activity be paused while it's reviewed.
Pro tip: Some states have their own charity care laws that go further than federal rules. Search your state name plus 'hospital charity care law'.

Step 3Follow up

  1. Call two weeks after sending to confirm it's complete.
  2. If approved, ask for refunds of anything you already paid that's now covered.
  3. If denied, ask for the reason in writing and whether you can provide more information.

Troubleshooting

If this happensDo this
Hospital says you earn too muchAsk about discounts for 'medically indigent' patients whose bills are large compared to income.
Bill already in collectionsApply anyway and ask the hospital to recall the account while you're reviewed.
