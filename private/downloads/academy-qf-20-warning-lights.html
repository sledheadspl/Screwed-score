Dashboard Warning Lights Decoded

What every common light means, how urgent it is, and what to check before calling anyone.

Time: 15 minutes to learnLevel: BeginnerSteps: 3

Dash lights follow a color code: red means stop or act now, yellow/amber means check soon, green or blue is just information. Knowing the handful that matter keeps you safe and stops you paying for checks you can do yourself.

Safety first, read before you start

  • Red oil pressure or red temperature light: pull over safely and turn the engine off. Driving on can destroy the engine.
  • Red brake light with a soft pedal: stop driving.
  • Flashing check engine light: slow down and get it checked soon.

  • The red lights that mean stop

What you need

  • Your owner's manual (its light chart is the final word for your car)
  • Tire gauge
  • OBD2 reader (optional)

Step 1Red: act now

  1. Oil pressure (oil can): stop, engine off, check the oil level. Don't restart if it's low or the light stays on.
  2. Temperature (thermometer): stop and let it cool. Check coolant only when cold. Turning on the heater full can shed heat on the way to a safe stop.
  3. Brake (!) or BRAKE: check the parking brake is fully off. If it is, check brake fluid; a soft pedal means don't drive.
  4. Battery/charging: the alternator isn't charging. You have limited time before the car dies. Turn off accessories and head to a safe place.

Step 2Amber: check soon

  1. Check engine: read the code (see the check-engine lesson). Solid is usually not urgent; flashing is.
  2. TPMS (horseshoe with !): check tire pressures cold.
  3. ABS: normal brakes still work, anti-lock doesn't. Get it checked.
  4. Airbag/SRS: airbags may not deploy in a crash. Get it checked promptly.
  5. Traction/stability (car with squiggles): flashing while driving is normal on slippery roads; solid means a fault or that it was switched off.

Step 3Information

  1. Green and blue lights (headlights, high beam, cruise, eco) are just status.
  2. Wrench or 'maint req': a service reminder, not a fault. Reset it after the service.

Troubleshooting

If this happensDo this
Several lights at once after a jump-startLow voltage confuses modules. Drive a while or charge the battery; check if they clear.
Light flickers on bumpsCan be low fluid sloshing (oil, brake) or a loose sensor connector.

Get it checked right away if

  • Any red light stays on after basic checks
  • Airbag light is on
  • Brake pedal feels soft or goes to the floor
  • Engine overheats more than once

Printable checklist

  • Know the 4 red stop lights
  • Know check engine solid vs flashing
  • Tire pressures if TPMS
  • Parking brake off if brake light
  • Owner's manual chart bookmarked
