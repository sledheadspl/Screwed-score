Weatherstrip Doors and Windows to Cut Drafts

Find the leaks, seal them and stop paying to heat the outdoors.

Time: 1 to 2 hoursLevel: BeginnerSteps: 3

Drafty doors and windows waste energy and money. Finding leaks is easy, and modern weatherstrip, door sweeps and caulk fix most of them in an afternoon.

Safety first, read before you start

  • Use a ladder properly for high windows.
  • Don't seal over gas appliance combustion air vents or required ventilation.

What you'll learn

  • Find drafts with simple tests
  • Replace door weatherstrip and sweeps
  • Seal window gaps
  • Choose the right product

What you need

  • Incense stick or a candle (careful) to spot drafts
  • Door weatherstrip matching the existing type
  • Door sweep
  • Exterior caulk and caulk gun
  • Utility knife, tape measure, screwdriver

Step 1Find the leaks

  1. On a windy day, move a lit incense stick slowly around door and window edges; the smoke bends at leaks.
  2. Close a door on a dollar bill. If it slides out easily, the seal there is weak.

Step 2Doors

  1. Pull out the old compression strip from the frame kerf (slot) and press in the new one.
  2. Replace a worn door sweep or adjust the threshold height so the sweep just touches.
  3. Check the door latches firmly; adjust the strike plate if not.

Step 3Windows

  1. Seal gaps between trim and wall outside with exterior caulk.
  2. Add self-adhesive foam or V-strip to sashes where they meet the frame (clean and dry surfaces first).
  3. For old single-pane windows, consider removable interior film kits for winter.

Troubleshooting

If this happensDo this
Door now hard to closeStrip too thick. Use a thinner profile.
Adhesive strip peelsSurface was dirty or cold. Clean with alcohol and apply above about 50 °F.

When to stop and call a pro

  • Rotted frames or sills
  • Window glass seal failure (fog between panes)

Printable checklist

  • Drafts located
  • Door strip replaced
  • Sweep fitted and touching
  • Strike adjusted
  • Outside trim caulked
  • Window strips applied clean and dry
