Cancel Any Subscription, Gym or Membership

Find every recurring charge and cancel cleanly, with proof, even when they fight back.

Time: 30 to 90 minutesLevel: BeginnerSteps: 4

Recurring charges hide in card statements, app stores and PayPal. Most cancel in a few clicks; some (gyms, certain memberships) make it hard. The key is a written record and knowing your card issuer can help.

Read this first

  • Cancel the service first, then block it at the bank only if they keep charging.
  • Read the contract for notice periods; some require 30 days' notice.
  • Keep screenshots and confirmation numbers.

What you'll learn

  • Find every recurring charge
  • Cancel through the right channel
  • Handle 'call to cancel' and gym traps
  • Stop charges that continue after cancelling

What you need

  • 3 months of bank and card statements
  • App store subscription pages (Apple, Google)
  • PayPal automatic payments page

Step 1Find them all

  1. Scan statements for repeating amounts.
  2. Check Apple (Settings > your name > Subscriptions) and Google Play (Payments & subscriptions).
  3. Check PayPal's automatic payments list.

Step 2Cancel the right way

  1. Cancel through the same place you signed up (app store subscriptions must be cancelled there).
  2. Screenshot the confirmation page and save the email.
  3. For 'call to cancel' services, note the date, time, rep name and confirmation number.

Step 3Gyms and hard cases

  1. Read the contract for the cancellation method (some require a letter).
  2. Send a written cancellation by certified mail or email with read receipt, quoting your member number.
  3. Many states have specific rules on gym/health club contracts; search your state name plus 'health club cancellation law'.

Step 4If charges continue

  1. Dispute the charge with your card issuer, attaching your cancellation proof (see the credit card dispute lesson).
  2. Ask your bank about stopping payments to that merchant.

Troubleshooting

If this happensDo this
No cancel buttonCheck the help center for 'cancel'; if none, email support and keep it.
Charged after cancellingSend proof to the merchant, then dispute with the card issuer.

Escalate if

  • They refuse to cancel: report to the FTC (reportfraud.ftc.gov) and your state attorney general
  • Charges continue after a dispute

Printable checklist

  • Statements scanned
  • App stores and PayPal checked
  • Each cancelled at source
  • Confirmations saved
  • Gym cancelled in writing
  • Next statement checked
