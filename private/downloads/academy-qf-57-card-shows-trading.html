Trade and Sell at Card Shows and Shops Without Getting Ripped Off

Prepare your list, know buy percentages and negotiate fair trades in person.

Time: 20 minutes prepLevel: BeginnerSteps: 4

In person, dealers need margin to stay in business, so they buy below market and sell at or above it. Knowing typical percentages and having current prices on your phone lets you negotiate fairly instead of guessing.

Read this first

  • Keep valuable cards on you, not left at someone's table.
  • Count cash in front of the dealer.
  • For trades, agree on the value basis (market price) before swapping.

What you need

  • A binder or box of what you're selling
  • Phone with price checks
  • A written list with current values

Step 1Prepare

  1. Price every card using recent sold data.
  2. Decide your minimum price for each before you go.

Step 2Know the percentages

  1. Cash offers are commonly a percentage of market; trade credit is often a higher percentage.
  2. Ask each dealer what percentage they pay before handing over cards.

Step 3Negotiate

  1. Show your sold-data numbers politely.
  2. Get offers from a few tables before deciding.
  3. Be ready to walk away.

Step 4Traps

  1. Bundled offers can hide a low price on one valuable card; ask for card-by-card pricing.
  2. In trades, check the other card's condition carefully.
