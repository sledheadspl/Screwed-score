Clean Your Dryer Vent and Prevent Fires

Clear lint from the trap, duct and outside vent the right way.

Time: 30 to 60 minutesLevel: BeginnerSteps: 3

Lint buildup in dryer ducts is a well-known cause of house fires, and a clogged vent makes loads take much longer. Long drying times or a hot laundry room are warning signs.

A vent cleaning service charges
$100 to $175
You'll spend

Safety first, read before you start

  • Unplug the dryer. For a gas dryer, turn off the gas valve and don't kink or stress the gas line when moving it.
  • Replace plastic or foil accordion ducts with rigid or semi-rigid metal duct; they trap lint and can burn.
  • Never use screws through the duct; they snag lint.

What you need

  • Dryer vent brush kit (drill-driven rods)
  • Vacuum
  • Screwdriver or nut driver for clamps
  • Foil tape (not cloth duct tape)

Step 1Disconnect

  1. Unplug the dryer (and shut the gas valve if gas). Pull it out carefully.
  2. Loosen the clamp and remove the duct from the dryer and the wall.

Step 2Clean

  1. Vacuum the lint trap slot as deep as you can reach.
  2. Run the brush through the duct and the wall pipe from inside, in short sections.
  3. Go outside and clear the vent hood flap; it should open freely when the dryer runs.

Step 3Reconnect and test

  1. Reattach the duct with clamps, sealing joints with foil tape. Keep it as short and straight as possible.
  2. Run the dryer and feel strong airflow outside.

Troubleshooting

If this happensDo this
Flap stays shut when runningStill clogged, or the flap is stuck. Clean again.
Clothes still take longCheck for a crushed duct behind the dryer.

When to stop and call a pro

  • Long or rooftop duct runs
  • Gas line connection concerns
  • Bird nests or damage in the wall duct

Printable checklist

  • Unplugged / gas off
  • Trap slot vacuumed
  • Duct brushed
  • Outside flap clear
  • Metal duct, no screws
  • Airflow checked outside
